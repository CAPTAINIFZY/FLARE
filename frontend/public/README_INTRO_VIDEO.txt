========================================================================
FLARE 3D CINEMATIC INTRO VIDEO GUIDE
========================================================================

Where to place your video file:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
File path: frontend/public/intro.mp4
(e.g., c:\Users\ACER\SIH\frontend\public\intro.mp4)

Recommended Video Specifications:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Format: MP4 (H.264 / AAC) or WebM (VP9)
• Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p) or 2560x1440 (1440p)
• Aspect Ratio: 16:9 widescreen
• Frame Rate: 30 fps or 60 fps
• Audio: Stereo AAC (starts muted for browser autoplay policy; user can click 'Sound On')
• Length: 10 to 30 seconds recommended

Features Included:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Auto-play on first visit with smooth fade-in
2. 3D canvas particle warp & flight radar background (displays automatically when video is loading or before video file is added)
3. Custom video picker ("Choose Video") right inside the player so you can test any video file from your computer immediately
4. Interactive HUD overlays, coordinates, flight radar telemetry
5. Play/Pause toggle (or press SPACE)
6. Sound On / Unmute with dynamic dancing audio equalizer bars (or press 'M')
7. Progress timeline scrubber
8. Skip Intro button (or press ESC) with futuristic audio chime
9. "3D Intro" button in Topbar and Dashboard to replay anytime during demonstrations and presentations
